# Page patterns

## Dashboard
Lead with a concise title and time or filter context, then show a small set of
decision-relevant metrics, trends, and an actionable recent-activity region.
Do not fill the page with decorative charts that do not support a decision.

## Data list
Use title and primary action, filters or search, table/list content, and
pagination or infinite loading. Define column priority and a mobile fallback.
Keep filters in the URL or existing state convention when the project supports
deep links.

## Detail and settings
Group related fields into sections, show current status and last-updated
context, and keep save/reset actions predictable. Warn before losing unsaved
changes when appropriate.

## Form and onboarding
Explain purpose before asking for input. Mark required fields, validate near
the field, summarize submit errors, preserve values, and make completion
feedback explicit.

## Conversation or AI workspace
Keep the prompt/input action obvious, show pending and streaming states,
preserve history, expose retry/cancel where possible, and make generated
content distinguishable from user input.

Choose the smallest pattern that fits the task; do not force a dashboard layout
onto a simple form or turn a short list into a complex table.
