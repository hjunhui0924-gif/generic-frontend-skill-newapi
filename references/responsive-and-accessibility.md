# Responsive and accessibility checks

Start from the narrow layout when the content is form-like; for data-heavy
views, define which columns are essential and provide a usable scroll or card
alternative. Do not hide the only way to complete a task at a breakpoint.

Use semantic elements, associated labels, logical heading order, visible
focus-visible styles, keyboard-operable menus/dialogs, and meaningful button
names. Keep touch targets usable, maintain readable contrast, and provide
status text in addition to color. Respect reduced motion and avoid focus loss
after dialogs, route changes, or validation.
